Du kannst deine Entscheidung jederzeit in den Einstellungen deines Geräts ändern (iOS: Einstellungen › Datenschutz › Tracking; Android: Einstellungen › Google › Werbung).

Google kann Daten auch in die USA übertragen. Google ist nach dem EU-US Data Privacy Framework zertifiziert. Weitere Informationen: policies.google.com/privacy und So nutzt Google Daten aus Apps unserer Partner.

Sway is an offline game. There are no user accounts, no registration and no chat. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data ourselves. Data is only processed by the third-party services listed below, which are needed to run the app and display advertising.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Google may process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only with your permission)
  • IP address (used for approximate location)
  • device and app information (e.g. device type, operating system, language, app version)
  • ad interactions (e.g. whether an ad was viewed or tapped)

Consent: Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked on first launch, via a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform), whether personalised ads may be shown. Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. On iPhone the app additionally asks for permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads).

Legal basis (GDPR): Art. 6(1)(a) (consent) for personalised ads; Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in funding a free app) for non-personalised ads.

Google may transfer data to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
